Back to Raw Motion: Latent-Free Diffusion Transformers for Text-to-Motion and Exact Conditional Generation

Yun Chen* Jeonghyeok Do* Munchurl Kim†

Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), South Korea

* Equal contribution† Corresponding authorcyruby@kaist.ac.krehwjdgur0913@kaist.ac.krmkimee@kaist.ac.kr

Paper arXiv Code Videos BibTeX
Abstract

As generative models tackle larger and more complex datasets, learned codecs enable efficient generation through compact latent representations, but may lose details and cannot guarantee exact preservation of given conditions. Generating directly in the original data space avoids errors introduced by the codec. Recent advances in image generation demonstrate that pixel diffusion can produce high-quality samples at practical computational costs. Inspired by these advances, we conduct a preliminary study of pixel diffusion, demonstrating its potential for adaptation to motion generation.

Building on these findings, we present RawMo, a framework that brings text-to-motion generation to raw motion space. Our Motion Diffusion Transformer (MiT) models local skeletal structures and whole-body dynamics, while refining motion across frames to improve temporal consistency. Working directly in raw motion space further allows our high-noise text-motion alignment loss (HiNCE) to improve text consistency without intermediate motion decoding. RawMo supports multiple motion-conditioning tasks with a single conditional model while preserving given motion conditions exactly. Experiments on HumanML3D and KIT-ML show the best text alignment among compared methods and competitive motion quality, with ablations confirming the benefits of MiT and HiNCE.
